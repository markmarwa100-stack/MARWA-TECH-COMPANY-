Frequently Asked Questions

What services does MARWA TECH offer?

We offer web development, software development, cybersecurity solutions, IT consulting, cloud services and network infrastructure. Contact our team to discuss what your organisation needs.

Can you build a website for my business?

Yes. We can discuss a website based on your business goals, required features and budget. Contact us with a description of what you would like to build.

Do you develop custom software?

We offer software development services. Share the problem you want the software to solve so our team can discuss the possible solution with you.

Can you help with office networks?

Yes. Network infrastructure is one of our service areas. Tell us about your office setup and the network support you are looking for.

Do you provide cybersecurity solutions?

We offer cybersecurity solutions. Contact us to explain your security concerns and discuss the support your organisation may require.

How do I request technical support?

Visit our Contact page and send us a message explaining the issue. Include relevant details so our team can understand what assistance you need.

How much do your services cost?

Costs depend on the type of service, project size and requirements. Contact us with your project details to discuss a suitable quotation.

Where does MARWA TECH operate?

Please contact our team for information about our service locations and whether we can support your particular project.
